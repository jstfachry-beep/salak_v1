A Space for Every Kind of Gathering

We provide 12 meeting rooms across the property, ready for functions from an intimate board discussion to a full-scale conference of hundreds of guests. Every room comes with audio-visual equipment, projector screens, and wireless microphones, with HD video conferencing and large display screens available on request.
